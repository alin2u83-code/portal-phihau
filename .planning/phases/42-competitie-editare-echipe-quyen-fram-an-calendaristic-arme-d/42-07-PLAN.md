<objective>
Make team-member editing safe and add the FRAM rule to the category modal.

Purpose: today both InscriereModal (edit mode, l.263-276) and Pas4Sumar (existing teams, l.376-384, delete error ignored) delete all members and re-insert them. With the new FRAM trigger (42-01) a re-insert of a member who lacks FRAM (a super admin exception, or FRAM annulled later) is refused, and the team would be left empty. A diff-based sync (add, re-role, remove) never re-inserts unchanged members and never deletes before the new rows exist (D-08). The modal is also an enrollment surface, so it must apply D-04/D-05 like Pas1 (42-05).
Super admin flag: usePermissions(useData().activeRoleContext).isSuperAdmin (project pattern, StagiiCompetitii.tsx:180).
Output: new service, modal and Pas4 updated.
</objective>
